Applied AI Research

AI-assisted qualitative research with evaluation discipline.

Research work focused on whether AI-generated personas and interview simulations can support qualitative research without losing methodological rigor.

The workflow combines custom GPT tools, Streamlit interfaces, transcript generation, comparison against human interview patterns, and Python NLP evaluation. The goal is not simply to generate synthetic text; it is to evaluate where LLM-assisted workflows are useful, where they distort evidence, and how researchers should interpret outputs responsibly.

  • AI-generated personas and founder interview simulations
  • Transcript comparison across AI-generated and human interview responses
  • Python NLP evaluation for thematic coding and sentiment scoring
  • Custom GPT and Streamlit workflow tools for repeatable research workflows
  • Responsible AI-assisted qualitative research methodology
